01About me

I'm a PhD candidate at the Department of Information Engineering of the University of Padova, where I started my doctorate in October 2023. My thesis, which I will defend in early 2027, mainly looks at how intelligent systems can make transportation fairer, more sustainable and more efficient. I'm part of the AMCO lab (Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Control) and work mainly on deep reinforcement learning, on both theory and applications.

From February to July 2025 I was a visiting researcher at the Automatic Control Laboratory (IfA) of ETH Zürich, working on mean-field reinforcement learning for artificial currency economies.

Before the PhD I earned my MSc in Control Systems Engineering (2023) and my BSc in Information Engineering (2021), both at the University of Padova. I also took part in the Bosch Future Mobility Challenge twice: as a team member in 2023 and as a mentor in 2024. Our team placed second both times.
